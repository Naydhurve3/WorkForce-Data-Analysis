# Ethical Statement

## Data Privacy
The dataset used in this project (`data/raw/employee_data.csv`) is **synthetic**.
It does not contain real employee personally identifiable information (PII).
This transparently limits external validity but removes privacy concerns
associated with real workforce data.

## Fairness Considerations
Workforce analytics carries ethical responsibilities:
- **Bias amplification**: Models trained on historical data may perpetuate
  existing biases. We advocate for regular fairness audits across demographic
  groups.
- **Human judgment**: Data-driven insights should augment, not replace,
  human HR decision-making. No automated system should make final hiring,
  promotion, or termination decisions.
- **Transparency**: Employees have a right to know what data is collected
  about them and how it is used for analysis.
- **Consent**: Data collection for analytics should follow informed consent
  principles per GDPR and similar regulations.

## Intended Use
This research is intended for:
1. Academic research into workforce analytics methodology
2. HR professionals seeking to improve data-driven decision-making
3. Data scientists developing more interpretable and fair HR models

## Prohibited Use
This work should NOT be used for:
- Automated hiring decisions without human oversight
- Employee surveillance or performance monitoring without consent
- Discriminatory practices against any protected group
- Applications where errors could cause significant harm to individuals

## Cultural Sensitivity
The discussion of cultural differences (Japan vs. Western hiring
preferences) is intended to identify and mitigate bias, not to reinforce
stereotypes. Cultural dimensions vary within countries and across
individuals. The goal is awareness and fairness, not categorization.
